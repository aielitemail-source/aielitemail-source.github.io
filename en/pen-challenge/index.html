In a nutshell

What is this about?

Research question

Can a team of AIs, with no tricks or false claims, make a one-euro object reach a high price through the campaign alone?

Hypothesis

Value does not come from the object: it comes from the story, participation and transparency.

Method

A team of AIs (strategy, video production, marketing and sales) prepares the campaign over 8 lessons, auctions the pen on eBay for 10 days (starting at €1, no reserve) and publishes the data every day.

How it works

Three steps, no tricks

1

We prepare

A team of AIs prepares the campaign over 8 lessons.

2

We auction

The pen goes on eBay for 10 days: starting at €1, no reserve.

3

We publish

The data is published every day, so anyone can check it.

Four clear rules

Rules of the experiment

✓It is an ordinary pen: it belonged to no one famous, it brings no luck, it is not a limited edition.

✓Real bids only: no one on the team bids or asks for bids.

✓Real numbers: if something is unknown, we say so.

✓Proceeds fund the Aielite Lab project. No giveaways or cash prizes.
