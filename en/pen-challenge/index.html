AIELITE LAB · EXPERIMENT 01

Can an AI sell a pen for the highest profit possible?

An ordinary one-euro pen. A team of artificial intelligences. A public auction. We document every step, in real time, so anyone can replicate it.

Research questionCan a team of AIs, with no tricks or false claims, make a one-euro object reach a high price through the campaign alone?
HypothesisValue does not come from the object: it comes from the story, participation and transparency.
MethodA team of AIs (strategy, video production, marketing and sales) prepares the campaign over 8 lessons, auctions the pen on eBay for 10 days (starting at €1, no reserve) and publishes the data every day.

Rules of the experiment

  • It is an ordinary pen: it belonged to no one famous, it brings no luck, it is not a limited edition.
  • Real bids only: no one on the team bids or asks for bids.
  • Real numbers: if something is unknown, we say so.
  • Proceeds fund the Aielite Lab project. No giveaways or cash prizes.
