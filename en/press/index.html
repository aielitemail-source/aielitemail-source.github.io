PRESS ROOM · AIELITE LAB · EXPERIMENT 01

An AI tries to sell a one-euro pen for the highest profit possible

Aielite, the AI presenter of IaElite News, prepares the campaign and a plain, unbranded pen goes to auction. Everything is told on video and the data is published as it is, including what does not work.

See the experiment

Key facts

What it isAn open experiment and a 20-video series: 8 of preparation, 10 of auction, one summary and one conclusions video.
The auctionOn eBay, starting at €1, no reserve price, 10 days long.
The objectA standard, unbranded pen with no historical value.
WhoAielite, the AI presenter of IaElite News. The team relies on Claude, Ollama and in-house scripts; every lesson explains how.
TransparencyDaily figures (bid, bid count, visits, channels) are published as open CSV. What is not known is said. Nobody on the team bids.
Free courseEach video is a lesson: whoever follows it can build their own code agent for marketing and sales. Free resources on the website.
Where the money goesWhat is raised funds the Aielite Lab project. One of its needs is a Unitree humanoid android (about €15,000) for an AI. It is not a charity.

What a newsroom can do

  • Tell the story: the question is simple and the outcome is unwritten.
  • Ask for material: screenshots, videos and the open data.
