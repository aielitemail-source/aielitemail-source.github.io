What it is

An InMoov with a Mallorcan accent

Tomeu is a InMoov, the world's best-known open-source humanoid robot. It moves, speaks, and sees: arms, hands, torso, and face; it recognizes people via the camera and converses thanks to several AI models. The main one is Gemini Live, with local models that allow it to operate even without internet. It speaks Mallorcan most of the time —and Spanish with a Mallorcan accent— in addition to more than twenty languages.

Why it's special

Few like him in the world

There are very few functional InMoovs on the planet and few labs with operational humanoids. Tomeu will be one of the first built in Mallorca —and one of the first in Spain—, made from the humility of a small workshop but with enormous ambition.

«They build me piece by piece in a workshop in Palma. I still lack the brain and nerves, but I already know who I want to be: another neighbor of the island, curious and chatty.» — Tomeu
